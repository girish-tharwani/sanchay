🚀 Welcome — let's get you set up
Before you start recording transactions, complete these three steps in order:
1
Add family members
Go to Profile → + Add Member. Add everyone in your household. Don't mark anyone as an earning member yet — you'll do that in step 3.
2
Add your bank accounts
Go to Accounts → Bank Accounts → + Add. Add the accounts where salaries and income are deposited. You can add credit cards and loans later.
3
Complete earnings details
Return to Profile and click the ₹ button next to each earning member. Mark them as earning and fill in their salary and income details.
